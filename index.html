How this works / limitations

This app runs Meta's open-source HT-Demucs (6-stem) music source-separation model fully client-side, using ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly/WebGPU). Your audio file never leaves your computer.

No publicly available separation model distinguishes lead vs rhythm guitar, or treats percussion and keyboards as fully separate stems from drums/piano — those finer splits don't exist in any open model today. HT-Demucs's six real outputs (drums, bass, other, vocals, guitar, piano) are the finest breakdown currently available, so the checkboxes above map onto them: percussion is folded into Drums, and lead/rhythm guitar come out as one combined Guitar stem.

The first time you click "Separate Stems," your browser downloads the AI model (roughly 130–260 MB depending on precision) from Hugging Face and caches it, so later runs skip the download. Processing itself happens in chunks and can take a few minutes per song since it's all running on your CPU/GPU in-browser rather than on a server.
