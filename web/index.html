How this works / limitations

This app runs Meta's open-source HT-Demucs (6-stem) music source-separation model locally via the official demucs Python package. Your audio file is processed entirely on this machine and never leaves it.

The extracted vocals stem is then optionally run through a second AI pass — a community "Karaoke" model from the UVR (Ultimate Vocal Remover) project — to split it further into Lead Vocals and Backing Vocals (harmonies/BGVs).

No publicly available open model distinguishes lead vs rhythm guitar, or treats percussion and keyboards as fully separate stems from drums/piano — those finer splits don't exist outside of proprietary commercial services (e.g. Moises.ai's paid API), which this app doesn't use since everything here runs locally and free. HT-Demucs's remaining outputs (drums, bass, other, guitar, piano) are the finest breakdown available locally, so percussion is folded into Drums and lead/rhythm guitar come out as one combined Guitar stem.

The first time you separate a song, the app downloads the AI models (roughly 300–500 MB for HT-Demucs, plus another few hundred MB the first time you request Lead/Backing Vocals) and caches them, so later runs skip the download. Processing speed depends on your CPU/GPU — expect anywhere from under a minute to a few minutes per song.
