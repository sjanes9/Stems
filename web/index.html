How this works / limitations

This app runs Meta's open-source HT-Demucs (6-stem) music source-separation model locally via the official demucs Python package. Your audio file is processed entirely on this machine and never leaves it.

No publicly available separation model distinguishes lead vs rhythm guitar, or treats percussion and keyboards as fully separate stems from drums/piano — those finer splits don't exist in any open model today. HT-Demucs's six real outputs (drums, bass, other, vocals, guitar, piano) are the finest breakdown currently available, so the checkboxes above map onto them: percussion is folded into Drums, and lead/rhythm guitar come out as one combined Guitar stem.

The first time you separate a song, the app downloads the AI model (roughly 300–500 MB) and caches it, so later runs skip the download. Processing speed depends on your CPU/GPU — expect anywhere from under a minute to a few minutes per song.
